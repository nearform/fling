Agents at scale

        
Your agents work while you sleep. They leave you links.

        
A run that ends in a folder of HTML is a run nobody looks at. Give each background agent an API key and let it publish its results, so the work arrives as something you can open, click through and comment on.

        

      

      

    

  


  
  

    

      
Safe to share by default

      
Easy to share. Hard to leak.

      

        

          
🔒Private until you say so

          
Every new site needs a sign-in through your organisation's identity provider. Only people your Fling admits get in.

          
private · sign-in required

        

        

          
🔗Share links when you want them

          
Need someone outside? Create a share link for one site. Replace it or revoke it whenever you like; the old one stops working.

          
no share link

        

        

          
🛑Secrets never ship

          
Every deploy is scanned for keys and tokens, and dotfiles like .env are never published. A finding stops the deploy; there's no override.

🗄️Yours to take down

          
Archive a site to take it offline and keep its files, bring it back later, or delete it with every version.
